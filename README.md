# Helicopter Mount

Scout in a single-seat Gyrocopter or carry a crew and their supplies across ICARUS in a transport helicopter.

**Requires Dangerous Horizons. Beta release, provided as-is.**

[Download Helicopter Mount 0.4.5](https://github.com/VariantCreator/Helicopter-Mount/releases/download/v0.4.5/Helicopter-Mount-0.4.5.zip) · [All releases](https://github.com/VariantCreator/Helicopter-Mount/releases) · [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/394)

## 📦 Installation

1. Close ICARUS. In Steam, right-click the game and select **Manage → Browse local files**.
2. Extract the main ZIP. Put `HelicopterMount_P.pak` in `Icarus\Content\Paks\mods`. Create the `mods` folder if needed.
3. When updating, replace the old PAK. Restart the game. For co-op, the host or dedicated server and every player need the same build.

## 🚁 Aircraft

Unlock the **Helicopter** in Tier 5 after the Polymerizer and craft it at the Manufacturer. It runs on Refined Oil and starts with one pilot seat, one passenger seat and 250 kg of cargo capacity. Two optional seats bring it up to four people and 750 kg.

Unlock the **Gyrocopter** in Tier 3 after the Biofuel Can and craft it at the Machining Bench, Fabricator or Manufacturer. It runs on Biofuel, carries one pilot and starts with six slots and 80 kg of cargo capacity. Give it a short takeoff run on level ground and keep forward speed in the air.

## 🛠️ This release

Added Cargo sorting and 15 paint schemes. Fixed parked Gyrocopters falling through the ground on reload. Packed aircraft show their names and restore your claim when redeployed. Helicopter audio is 20% quieter.

Built for **ICARUS 3.0.37**. Examine either aircraft to change its **Paint Scheme** or **Sort** regular Cargo. Heavy Cargo keeps its own order.

## 🔊 Optional Starlink audio

If the rotor and engine sounds are missing after a **Starlink 4.3.0** merge, close the game and extract the [Starlink Audio ZIP](https://github.com/VariantCreator/Helicopter-Mount/releases/download/v0.4.5/Helicopter-Mount-0.4.5-Starlink-Audio.zip). Copy `HelicopterMount.bank` and `GyrocopterMount.bank` into `Icarus\Content\FMOD\Desktop` and restart ICARUS. Each affected player installs the files locally.
